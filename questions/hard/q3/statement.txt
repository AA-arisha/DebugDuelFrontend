In the fractured Paradox-Verse, crafting is no longer a simple task — it is a ritual that stabilizes reality itself. Paradox-Verse Kiro begins with exactly one fragile stick. To prevent the Red Star from tearing the timelines apart, he must craft at least k torches. Each torch requires exactly one stick and one coal.
A wandering trader appears between dimensions and offers two repeatable trades:

Trade A: Exchange 1 stick to receive x sticks. (You lose 1 stick and gain x sticks.)
Trade B: Exchange y sticks to receive 1 coal. (You lose y sticks and gain 1 coal.)

During a single trade, you may perform only one of these offers. You may use either trade any number of times and in any order.
Kiro starts with 1 stick and 0 coal. After gathering enough resources, he crafts torches, where each torch consumes exactly 1 stick and 1 coal. Your task is to determine the minimum number of trades required to craft at least k torches.
It is guaranteed that crafting at least k torches is always possible under the given constraints.
